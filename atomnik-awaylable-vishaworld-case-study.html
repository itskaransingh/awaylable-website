The Problem

One inbox. One person. Thousands of customers, waiting.

Vishaworld had built something genuinely valuable — a destination for engineers, hobbyists, and builders looking for quality components. The traffic was real. The intent to buy was real. The problem was entirely structural.

Every customer query landed in the same chat window, managed by one team member. Order status sat next to spec questions. Stock inquiries sat next to return requests. All waiting for the same pair of hands, in no particular order.

The math simply didn't work. An estimated 80% of queries were completely routine — questions with clear, consistent answers. But they consumed the same time and attention as complex ones, because every message had to be read and typed out by hand.

By the time the queue caught up, some customers had already moved on. Not because the product wasn't right — but because the silence felt like indifference.

The Solution

Awaylable — the assistant that works every hour Vishaworld doesn't.

Atomnik deployed Awaylable directly into Vishaworld's website — turning a static chat icon into an intelligent assistant capable of handling every routine query the moment it arrives, at any time of day, without a single human needing to step in.

Awaylable was trained on Vishaworld's actual product catalogue, stock data, shipping timelines, and return policies. When a customer asks "Do you have the ESP32 module in stock?" they get an answer in seconds. When someone asks about their order, the bot handles it. It doesn't feel like a generic chatbot — it feels like Vishaworld knows its products cold.

When a query genuinely requires a human decision, Awaylable escalates it cleanly — with the full conversation already captured, so no one wastes time repeating themselves. The team member who was previously spending 3 hours a day now spends about 20 minutes, on only the queries that actually need them.

Beyond answering questions, Awaylable also took on cart management and checkout — guiding customers from product discovery to purchase within a single conversation, with no friction and no drop-off.
